\section{Prework}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item What is an IC and SI engine?

	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item IC Engine: IC or Internal Combustion Engine is a heat engine that burns fuel inside a confined combustion chamber.
		      \item SI Engine: SI or Spark Ignition Engine is a specific type of IC engine that uses an electric spark to ignite that fuel.
	      \end{itemize}

	\item Explain the following: Swept volume, TDC, BDC, compression ratio, and clearance volume.

	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item Swept Volume: Swept volume or displacement volume is the volume displaced by piston as it travels from BDC to TDC in a single stroke.
		      \item TDC: TDC or Top Dead Center is the highest point the piston reaches inside the cylinder.
		      \item BDC: BDC or Bottom Dead Center is the lowest point the piston reaches inside the cylinder.
		      \item Compression Ratio: Compression ratio is the ratio of the total cylinder volume when the piston is at BDC to the clearance volume when the piston is at TDC.
		      \item Clearance Volume: Clearance volume is the small pocket of space remaining between the top of the piston and the cylinder head when the piston is at TDC.
	      \end{itemize}

	\item What is a stroke?

	      A stroke is one complete, full-length travel of the piston inside the cylinder in a single direction either all the way from TDC to BDC or BDC to TDC.


	\item How many valves are there in a four-stroke petrol engine? Name them.

	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item Inlet Valve or Intake Valve
		      \item Exhaust Valve
	      \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
