\section{Theory}

The internal combustion engine works on the combustion reaction that occur inside the piston cylinders of an engine. Depending on the ignition protocol, these engine can be divided into two types: SI or Spark Ignition and CI or Compression Ignition engine.

The name SI or Spark Ignition says what it does, air-fuel mixture ignited by spark using the spark plug. The common example of thes kind of engine is Petrol engine. On the other hand, the CI or Compression Ignition works a bit differently, initially compressing the air-fuel mixture and then ignited by spark using the spark plug.

After the ignition, the compressed air push or move the piston, transferring, the thermal energy into mechanical energy using the piston rod. The motion occured here is linear motion and the cycle known as otto cycle.

Petrol engine can be divided into two types, four-stroke and two-stroke engine. A four stroke engine compose of the four cycles inclduing intake, compression, power and exhaust cycle. On the contrary, a two-stroke engine compose of two cycles.

The efficiency or performance can be depend on many factors including fuel consumptions and emissions, speed and others.
