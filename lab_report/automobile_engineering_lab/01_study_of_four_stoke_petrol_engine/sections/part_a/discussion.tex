\section{Discussion}

The overall experience of the lab was good. We completed all the steps necessary to closely observe an engine.

Though everything was completed successfully, we noticed some important issues. First of all, there were no hand gloves. Working with an engine requires proper safety measures. So, we need gloves to protect our hands. Secondly, there was a lack of enough instructors. Only one instructor was managing the three groups simultaneously, causing a burden and interruption for the other groups.
