\section{Theory}

A diesel engine is an internal combustion engine that uses compression ignition: it burns fuel by compressing air until it's hot enough to ignite injected diesel fuel, no spark plug required. It has four-stroke cycle including intake stroke, compression stroke, power stroke, and exhaust stroke.

Diesel engine has several advantages including high fuel efficiency, longevity, lower CO and $CO_2$, fuel safety. It also has some disadvantages including higher $NO_X$, expensive to build, noise, maintenance and so on.
