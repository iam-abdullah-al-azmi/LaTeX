\begin{enumerate}

	\item What are cams and crankshafts?

	      A cam is rotating or sliding element that produces a specific motion in another element called follower. On the other hand, a crankshaft, is a rotating element that convert the reciprocating motion into rotational motion, and vice versa.

	\item Explain the concept of the compression ratio in a diesel engine and its impact on performance.

	      Compression ratio of a diesel engine is the ratio of the maximum cylinder volume (when the piston is at BDC) to the minimum cylinder volume (when the piston at TDC).

	      \begin{equation*}

		      Compression Ratio = \frac{V_BDC}{V_TDC}

	      \end{equation*}

	      The compression ratio has several impact on the performance of a diesel engine. Increasing the compression ratio, increases the thermal efficiency and reduce the fuel consumption and so on.
