\section{Assignment}

\begin{enumerate}

	\item Compare the petrol and diesel engine.

	      \begin{center}
		      \begin{tabular} {|l|l|l|}
			      \hline
			      \textbf{Feature}           & \textbf{Diesel Engine} & \textbf{Petrol Engine}     \\
			      \hline
			      \textbf{Intake Charge}     & Pure air only          & Pre-mixed air-fuel mixture \\
			      \textbf{Fuel Injection}    & Ultra-high pressure    & Low-to-moderate pressure   \\
			      \textbf{Ignition System}   & Compression ignition   & Spark ignition             \\
			      \textbf{Airflow Control}   & Unthrottle air intake  & Throttled body             \\
			      \textbf{Compression Ratio} & Higher                 & Lower                      \\
			      \hline
		      \end{tabular}
	      \end{center}

	\item What is compression ignition, and how does it occur in a diesel engine?

	      Compression ignition is a combustion process in which fuel ignites spontaneously from the extreme heat generated by rapidly compressing air inside a cylinder.

	\item Which components of the engine do the cylinder head, cylinder block, and crankcase house/contain respectively? Explain.

	      The cylinder head contains the valvetrain and combustion chamber tops. The cylinder block contains the cylinders and pistons and the cylinder crankcase contains contains the crankshaft and connecting rod.

	\item How do you identify a diesel engine?

	      There is many ways to identify a diesel engine. Among them, few examples are fuel filler neck, no spark plugs, higher pressure fuel system, heavy cast-iron block.

	\item Briefly describe the diesel cycle and relate it to the diesel engine.

	      The diesel cycle is an idealized air-standard thermodynamic cycle for compression-ignition engines. It has four processes:

	      \begin{enumerate}

		      \item Isentropic compression
		      \item Constant-pressure heat addition
		      \item Isentropic expansion
		      \item Constant-volume heat rejection

	      \end{enumerate}

	      The diesel cycle is the ideal model and the real diesel engine realizes it through compression ignition and high-pressure fuel injection.

	\item Describe what happens when you use diesel fuel in petrol engine.

	      Diesel in a petrol engine mainly fails to ignite properly (poor volatility, high ignition temperature), causing misfire, smoke, plug fouling, and carbon buildup, plus fuel-system and emissions damage, so it should be drained and flushed rather than run.

	\item State at which location fuel injectors spray inside diesel engine.

	      Diesel injectors spray into the combustion chamber at the top of the cylinder, directly above the piston, directly into the cylinder in DI engines, or into a pre-chamber in IDI engines.

	\item What enters the cylinder during the intake stroke.

	      In a diesel engine, air alone enters during the intake stroke; in a conventional petrol engine, an air-fuel mixture enters (or air only, in direct-injection petrol engines).

\end{enumerate}
